Complete Digital Risk Protection

Five modules designed to help identify, understand, and reduce external digital risks.

🛡️

Brand Protection

Protecting a brand's online identity

Helps businesses guard their online reputation against fake websites, impersonated social profiles, and misuse of logos.

  • Spot potential fake websites
  • Identify suspicious brand-name use
  • Monitor online reputation
  • Highlight impersonation warning signs
🎣

Phishing Detection

Recognizing fraudulent links and messages

Helps users recognize links that may try to steal passwords, OTPs, banking details, or confidential information.

  • Review suspicious URLs
  • Identify common phishing patterns
  • Highlight unusual links or wording
  • Offer safety recommendations
🔍

Data Leak Monitor

Awareness of potential data exposure

Supports awareness of exposed emails, credentials, customer records, or confidential documents when connected to trusted data sources.

  • Track potential data exposure
  • Identify sensitive information risks
  • Generate exposure alerts
  • Recommend protective actions
👤

VIP Protection

Digital protection for high-profile individuals

Helps executives, public figures, and professionals assess impersonation and targeted-attack risks.

  • Identify possible impersonation
  • Review suspicious messages
  • Highlight personal-data exposure risks
  • Suggest tailored security steps
🌐

Dark Web Intelligence

Awareness of emerging threat indicators

Uses relevant, lawful threat-intelligence sources to help organizations understand potential credential or data exposure.

  • Review reported data exposures
  • Check compromised-credential alerts through trusted services
  • Monitor threat indicators
  • Provide response recommendations

How It Works

A simple three-step approach to managing external digital risks.

01

Discover

Map your public digital footprint, including domains, social profiles, public mentions, and exposed assets.

02

Detect

Look for lookalike domains, phishing indicators, fake profiles, and data-exposure alerts from trusted sources.

03

Protect

Prioritize risks, review alerts, and take action to reduce exposure and protect digital trust.

The Impact of Digital Risk Protection

Finding warning signs early can help people and organizations respond more effectively.

✅ Customer Trust

Responding to fake websites helps customers find legitimate services.

📉 Reduced Fraud Risk

Early phishing awareness can reduce the chance of financial loss.

🛡️ Brand Reputation

Protecting online identity supports long-term credibility.

⚡ Faster Response

Clear alerts and recommendations help teams act promptly.
